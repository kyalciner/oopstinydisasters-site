Privacy

Oops! Tiny Disasters is an iPhone game. This page says what the game stores. It was last updated on 23 October 2026.

Account

You sign in with email, Apple, or Google. The account lives on Supabase. We store the user id, email address, and the sign-in session on your phone.

What you play

Your nickname, avatar, coins, scores, streaks, and friend list are saved with that account so they follow you to another phone. A nickname cannot be used by two accounts.

Live 1v1

A live match uses your signed-in account to pair you with another player. The match needs your score and how fast you tapped. It does not need your contacts.

Purchases

Coin packs and other items are sold by Apple. RevenueCat records the purchase and the COINS balance. The game does not see your card number.

Ads

The game can show Google AdMob ads, including rewarded ads and ads between rounds. You can buy No Ads to turn those off. Google’s own policy covers what an ad collects: policies.google.com/privacy.

Game Center

Scores and achievements can be sent to Apple Game Center. That uses the Apple ID signed in on the phone.

Analytics

If analytics is on in Settings, the game may send event names plus the round and level. Those events do not include your email or nickname. Turn analytics off in Settings and new events stop.

On the phone

Settings, sound, and a local copy of your progress stay on the device. Reset data in Settings wipes that local copy. It does not delete the account on the server.

Delete the account

Email support@oopstinydisasters.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We delete the Supabase profile, including nickname, scores, coins, and friends.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Do not sign up with a child’s email.

Contact

Questions about this page go to support@oopstinydisasters.com.
