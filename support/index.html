Support

Write to support@oopstinydisasters.com. Include your nickname, iPhone model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

Email support

Sign-in mail

Confirmation mail comes from Oops! Tiny Disasters. Open the link on the iPhone that has the game. One phone can request 5 of those emails.

A purchase

Coin packs are restored by Apple. If the coins do not show after a successful purchase, email support with the date and the pack name. Do not send a receipt screenshot that shows a card number.

Delete an account

Sign out in Settings if you only want to leave this phone. To delete the account on the server, email support from the address you used to sign up.

Privacy
